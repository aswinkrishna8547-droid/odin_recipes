Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a quintessential Italian comfort food consisting of stacked layers of wide pasta sheets, rich meat or vegetable ragù, and a creamy cheese filling, all baked together until bubbling and golden-brown. This beloved casserole typically features a deeply savory tomato-based sauce alongside layers of creamy ricotta, melted mozzarella, and sharp Parmesan cheese, creating an incredibly decadent and satisfying meal.

What makes this dish an enduring favorite worldwide is the magical way the layers meld together in the oven. As it bakes, the pasta absorbs the flavors of the robust sauce and rich cheeses, resulting in a perfect balance of textures that is ideal for family Sunday dinners or holiday gatherings.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles: 12 to 15 sheets (regular or no-boil)
  • Ground beef: 1 pound (or a mix of ground beef and Italian sausage)
  • Crushed tomatoes: 1 can (28 ounces)
  • Tomato paste: 2 tablespoons
  • Ricotta cheese: 15 ounces (whole milk preferred)
  • Mozzarella cheese: 4 cups, shredded (divided)
  • Parmesan cheese: 1/2 cup, freshly grated
  • Egg: 1 large, beaten (to bind the cheese filling)
  • Onion: 1 medium, finely chopped
  • Garlic: 3 cloves, minced
  • Olive oil: 1 tablespoon
  • Italian seasoning: 2 teaspoons
  • Salt and black pepper: To taste

Steps

  1. Cook the meat and aromatics: Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat, add the chopped onion and minced garlic, and sauté for 3 minutes before crumbling in the ground beef to brown completely.
  2. Simmer the ragù sauce: Stir the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper into the meat, bringing it to a low simmer for 20 to 30 minutes to develop a thick, rich sauce.
  3. Boil the noodles: Cook the lasagna sheets in a large pot of boiling salted water according to the package instructions until al dente, then drain and lay them flat on parchment paper to prevent sticking.
  4. Mix the cheese filling: Combine the ricotta cheese, one beaten egg, half of the grated Parmesan cheese, and a pinch of salt and pepper in a medium bowl, mixing until completely smooth.
  5. Preheat the oven: Set your oven to 375°F (190°C) so it reaches the correct baking temperature while you assemble the layers.
  6. Begin the assembly: Spread a thin layer of the meat ragù across the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish to prevent the pasta from sticking.
  7. Layer the pasta and cheese: Arrange 3 to 4 lasagna noodles over the sauce, spread a third of the ricotta cheese mixture evenly over the noodles, and sprinkle with a generous handful of shredded mozzarella.
  8. Repeat the layers: Repeat the sequence—meat sauce, noodles, ricotta mixture, and mozzarella cheese—two more times until you reach the final top layer of pasta.
  9. Add the final top layer: Spread the remaining meat ragù over the top layer of noodles and cover it completely with the rest of the mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  10. Bake covered then uncovered: Cover the dish tightly with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes, then remove the foil and bake uncovered for another 15 minutes until the cheese is bubbling and golden-brown.
  11. Rest before slicing: Remove the lasagna from the oven and let it rest for 15 to 20 minutes so the layers firm up, ensuring perfect, clean slices when served.
