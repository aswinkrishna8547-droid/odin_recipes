Vegetable Pot Pie

Description

Vegetable Pot Pie is the ultimate comfort food, featuring a rich, savory medley of fresh vegetables smothered in a creamy, herbed gravy and baked beneath a golden, flaky pastry crust. This hearty dish typically packs a colorful mix of potatoes, carrots, peas, corn, and celery, making it a wholesome and deeply satisfying meal for cozy weeknights or holiday gatherings.

What makes this recipe a timeless favorite is its incredible versatility and rustic appeal. You can easily customize the filling with your favorite seasonal vegetables or clean out your crisper drawer, while the contrast between the velvety filling and the crisp, buttery crust provides a perfect texture in every single bite.

Ingredients

  • Pie crusts: 2 sheets (refrigerated unbaked store-bought or homemade)
  • Potatoes: 1 large russet or Yukon Gold, peeled and diced small
  • Carrots: 2 medium, diced
  • Celery: 2 stalks, sliced
  • Frozen peas and corn mix: 1 cup
  • Onion: 1 small, finely chopped
  • Garlic: 2 cloves, minced
  • Butter: 1/4 cup (or plant-based butter alternative)
  • All-purpose flour: 1/4 cup (to thicken the gravy)
  • Vegetable broth: 2 cups
  • Heavy cream: 1/2 cup (or full-fat coconut milk for a vegan alternative)
  • Dried thyme: 1/2 teaspoon
  • Salt and black pepper: To taste

Steps

  1. Preheat oven: Set your oven to 400°F (200°C) and let it warm up.
  2. Parboil potatoes and carrots: Place diced potatoes and carrots in a pot of salted water, bring to a boil, cook for 5 minutes until slightly tender, then drain and set aside.
  3. Sauté aromatics: Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat, add the onion and celery, and sauté for 5 minutes until soft before adding the minced garlic for another minute.
  4. Make the roux: Sprinkle the flour over the sautéed vegetables, stirring constantly for 1 to 2 minutes to cook out the raw flour taste.
  5. Simmer the gravy: Slowly whisk in the vegetable broth and heavy cream, bringing the mixture to a simmer until it thickens into a smooth, velvety gravy.
  6. Mix the filling: Stir the parboiled potatoes and carrots, frozen peas, frozen corn, dried thyme, salt, and black pepper into the gravy until well combined.
  7. Prepare the bottom crust: Press one sheet of the unbaked pie crust into the bottom of a 9-inch pie dish.
  8. Fill the pie: Pour the warm vegetable and gravy filling into the prepared pie crust.
  9. Top the pie: Place the second pie crust over the filling, crimp the edges to seal, and cut a few small slits in the top center to allow steam to escape.
  10. Bake until golden: Place in the oven and bake for 30 to 35 minutes until the pastry is completely golden-brown and the filling is bubbling.
  11. Cool and serve: Let the pie rest for 10 to 15 minutes before slicing so the filling sets perfectly.
