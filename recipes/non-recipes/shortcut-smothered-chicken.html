Shortcut Smothered Chicken

Description

Shortcut Smothered Chicken is a quick, comforting twist on a Southern classic that delivers rich, soulful flavor without hours of labor. This weekday-friendly recipe features juicy, pan-seared chicken breasts or thighs nestled in a velvety, deeply savory gravy. By utilizing smart shortcuts—like high-quality store-bought broth, pre-sliced mushrooms, or a quick flour slurry—you get a thick, luscious sauce that tastes like it simmered all afternoon in just a fraction of the time.

Perfect for busy weeknights, this dish transforms basic pantry staples into a decadent, lip-smacking meal. The chicken absorbs the rich notes of the gravy as it simmers, ensuring every single bite stays tender and incredibly flavorful. It is traditionally served hot over a bed of fluffy mashed potatoes, steamed white rice, or buttered egg noodles to catch every drop of the delicious smothered sauce.

Ingredients

  • Chicken breasts or thighs: 4 boneless, skinless cuts (pounded to even thickness)
  • Mushrooms: 8 ounces, pre-sliced (cremini or white button)
  • Onion: 1 medium, thinly sliced
  • Garlic: 3 cloves, minced
  • Olive oil: 1 tablespoon
  • Butter: 2 tablespoons
  • Chicken broth: 1.5 cups (high-quality store-bought)
  • All-purpose flour: 2 tablespoons (for the quick thickening slurry)
  • Garlic powder: 1 teaspoon (for seasoning the chicken)
  • Onion powder: 1 teaspoon (for seasoning the chicken)
  • Paprika: 1/2 teaspoon
  • Salt and black pepper: To taste

Steps

  1. Season the chicken: Pat the chicken cuts dry and season both sides generously with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper.
  2. Sear the chicken: Heat olive oil and one tablespoon of butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat, then sear the chicken for 5 to 6 minutes per side until golden brown, removing them to a plate (they will finish cooking later).
  3. Sauté mushrooms and onions: Melt the remaining tablespoon of butter in the same skillet, then add the sliced mushrooms and onions, cooking for 5 to 7 minutes until tender and browned.
  4. Add garlic: Stir the minced garlic into the skillet and cook for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  5. Pour the broth: Pour the chicken broth into the skillet, using a wooden spoon to scrape up all the delicious browned bits stuck to the bottom of the pan.
  6. Make the shortcut slurry: Whisk the flour into 2 tablespoons of cold water in a small bowl until completely smooth, then slowly pour it into the simmering broth while whisking continuously.
  7. Thicken the gravy: Let the gravy simmer for 2 to 3 minutes until it begins to bubble and thicken into a smooth sauce.
  8. Simmer and finish: Return the chicken and any rested juices back into the skillet, coat them in the gravy, and simmer for another 5 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and tender.
  9. Garnish and serve: Remove from heat, season the gravy with extra salt and pepper if needed, and serve hot over mashed potatoes or rice.
