Classic Meatloaf

Description

Classic Meatloaf is a quintessential American comfort food that delivers an old-school, nostalgic dinner experience. It features a perfectly seasoned blend of ground beef, aromatics, breadcrumbs, and herbs, all bound together and shaped into a tender, juicy loaf. Baked to savory perfection, it strikes the ideal balance between a rich, melt-in-your-mouth interior and a deeply flavorful exterior.

The crown jewel of this timeless recipe is the sweet and tangy tomato-based glaze brushed over the top, which caramelizes beautifully in the oven as it bakes. This dish is celebrated for its simplicity, crowd-pleasing appeal, and the fact that it yields spectacular leftovers that make the ultimate next-day meatloaf sandwiches.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef: 1.5 to 2 pounds (85% lean preferred)
  • Breadcrumbs: 1/2 cup (plain or Italian seasoned)
  • Onion: 1 small, finely diced
  • Garlic: 2 cloves, minced
  • Eggs: 2 large, beaten
  • Milk: 1/4 cup
  • Ketchup: 1/2 cup (divided for mixture and glaze)
  • Worcestershire sauce: 2 tablespoons
  • Brown sugar: 1 tablespoon (for the glaze)
  • Mustard: 1 teaspoon (yellow or Dijon)
  • Salt and pepper: To taste
  • Fresh parsley: 2 tablespoons, chopped (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven: Set your oven to 375°F (190°C) and lightly grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan or line a rimmed baking sheet with aluminum foil.
  2. Sauté the aromatics: Heat a small splash of oil in a skillet over medium heat, add the finely diced onion and minced garlic, and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until soft and translucent, then set aside to cool slightly.
  3. Mix the liquid ingredients: Whisk the eggs, milk, 1/4 cup of the ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, yellow mustard, salt, and black pepper together in a large bowl.
  4. Combine the meat mixture: Add the ground beef, breadcrumbs, sautéed onions and garlic, and fresh parsley to the bowl, using your hands to gently combine everything until just mixed without overworking the meat.
  5. Shape the loaf: Press the meat mixture evenly into your prepared loaf pan, or shape it into a uniform 9x5-inch log directly onto the lined baking sheet.
  6. Prepare the sweet glaze: Stir the remaining 1/4 cup of ketchup and the tablespoon of brown sugar together in a small bowl until smooth.
  7. Apply the glaze: Brush the sweet tomato glaze evenly over the top and sides of the shaped meatloaf before it goes into the oven.
  8. Bake the meatloaf: Place the loaf in the preheated oven and bake uncovered for 55 to 60 minutes until the top is beautifully caramelized and the internal temperature reaches 160°F (71°C).
  9. Rest and slice: Remove the meatloaf from the oven and let it rest for 10 minutes before slicing to ensure it holds its shape perfectly when served.
