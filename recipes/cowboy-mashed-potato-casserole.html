Cowboy Mashed Potato Casserole

Description

Cowboy Mashed Potato Casserole is a hearty, all-in-one comfort meal that packs bold, rustic flavors into a single baking dish. This crowd-pleasing casserole features a savory, deeply satisfying base of seasoned ground beef, corn, and black beans tossed in a smoky barbecue or tomato-based gravy, which is then topped with a thick, velvety layer of creamy mashed potatoes.

To finish it off, the dish is smothered in a generous layer of melted cheddar cheese and crisp bacon bits before being baked to bubbling perfection. It is the ultimate cold-weather meal or potluck favorite, combining the smoky elements of a classic cowboy dinner with the rich, soothing textures of a traditional shepherd's pie.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef: 1.5 pounds (lean ground beef preferred)
  • Mashed potatoes: 4 cups (prepared, creamy, and warm)
  • Cheddar cheese: 2 cups, shredded (divided)
  • Bacon: 6 strips, cooked and crumbled
  • Onion: 1 medium, chopped
  • Garlic: 2 cloves, minced
  • Corn: 1 cup (canned, drained, or frozen)
  • Black beans: 1 can (15 ounces), rinsed and drained
  • Barbecue sauce: 1/2 cup (or tomato-based savory gravy)
  • Worcestershire sauce: 1 tablespoon
  • Salt and black pepper: To taste
  • Green onions: 1/4 cup, sliced (for garnish)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven: Set your oven to 375°F (190°C) and grease a 9x13-inch baking dish with cooking spray or butter.
  2. Brown the meat and onion: Cook the ground beef and chopped onion in a large skillet over medium-high heat for 6 to 8 minutes, breaking the meat apart with a spoon until completely browned, then drain any excess grease.
  3. Add the garlic: Stir the minced garlic into the meat mixture and cook for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Mix the filling: Add the corn, rinsed black beans, barbecue sauce, and Worcestershire sauce to the skillet, stirring well, and let it simmer on low for 3 to 4 minutes until hot.
  5. Season the beef: Taste the beef mixture and season with salt and black pepper according to your preference.
  6. Transfer to the baking dish: Pour the savory ground beef and bean filling into the bottom of your prepared baking dish, spreading it out into an even layer.
  7. Spread the mashed potatoes: Gently spread the warm mashed potatoes over the top of the beef layer using a spatula to coat it completely from edge to edge.
  8. Top with cheese and bacon: Sprinkle the shredded cheddar cheese evenly over the mashed potatoes, then scatter the crumbled bacon pieces across the top.
  9. Bake until bubbling: Place the dish in the preheated oven and bake for 20 to 25 minutes until the cheese is completely melted, golden-brown, and the edges are bubbling.
  10. Garnish and serve: Remove the casserole from the oven, top with the sliced green onions, and let it stand for 5 minutes before scooping and serving.
